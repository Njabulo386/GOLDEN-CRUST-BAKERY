About Golden Crust Bakery

Thandi and Pieter Naidoo opened our first small shop in Melville in 2012 with one oven and a family sourdough starter. Today we bake for three Johannesburg communities while keeping the same slow, careful methods.

To bake honest, high-quality food that brings people together every day.

To be Johannesburg's most trusted neighbourhood bakery.

  • Fresh daily
  • Local ingredients
  • Community first
